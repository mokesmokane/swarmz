# Identify a tile (design)

Date: 2026-09-25. Adds to the sidebar and the windows and layouts spec.

- **A settings menu per row.** A ⋯ button on each sidebar row opens the tile's menu:
  Identify, Identify all tiles, Open in a new window, Rename…, Edit title…, and Stop and remove.
- **Identify.** The tile's window comes forward and its tab shows (when it is open somewhere),
  and for 3.5 s a large label over its pane gives its title, its Mac and its folder; its tab and
  its sidebar row are ringed in amber. A tile not open in any window is not opened: its row says
  "not open in a window".
- **Identify all tiles.** Every tile gets a number in the order the sidebar lists them; the
  number shows big over each visible pane, on each tab (so a hidden tab is found too) and on each
  row, for 3.5 s.
- Other windows show the labels too (the identify state is part of their mirror).
